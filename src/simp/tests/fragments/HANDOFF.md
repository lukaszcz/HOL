# TASK_15 fragment fixture handoff

The authoritative task handoff is
[`../compatibility/TASK_15_HANDOFF.md`](../compatibility/TASK_15_HANDOFF.md).

- `run_fragments.py`: paired ordinary fragment behavior/proofs and retained
  graphs at 0, 1, 10, 100, 1,000 and 10,000 rules; optional consumed-result
  construction timings. Adaptive and sealed matched-count modes are separate.
- `routes/`: additional paired printing, history, exclusion/force-add,
  duplicate, AC/maker/conversion/collector and exception-order observations.
- `assembly/`: emitted native work and optimized caller/callee inspection;
  the strict local assembly comparator deliberately reports merge's real
  added caller instruction. Its compiler tree evidence resolves that change
  as argument materialization moved out of a removed wrapper, not extra work.
- `summarize.py`: preserves all per-workload timing decisions and controls,
  including red/unresolved default full-module rows. An exit of zero is not
  a final whole-module performance acceptance claim.

`Holmake check` runs the graph/behavior and route gates in private processes;
`src/simp/src/Holmakefile` includes this at `HOLSELFTESTLEVEL=1`.
Timing and assembly diagnostics remain explicit, separate targets. The
extension suites retain their ordinary normal-selftest integration.

Earlier pre-restoration/protocol-control receipts under `evidence/` are
historical negative-control evidence, not final task passes. All final inputs,
commands, observations, source seals, decisions and scope limitations are
identified by the authoritative handoff. Never substitute the matched
registry/context diagnostic for ordinary-client whole-module acceptance.
